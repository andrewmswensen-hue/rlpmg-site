## What do these comparison pages cover?

Three decisions, one page each. What a management structure costs over a year. Whether hiring a manager beats doing the work. How RL Property Management's published terms line up against the model most Columbus, Ohio managers use. Each page shows the arithmetic rather than the adjectives, and each carries its own verification date.

- [Flat fee vs. percentage property management](/compare/flat-fee-vs-percentage-property-management/): annual totals at rents of $1,200, $1,500, $1,800, $2,200, and $2,800, in a leasing year and in a steady-state year, including the $250 renewal fee and the $125 annual accounting and technology fee.
- [Self-managing vs. hiring a property manager](/compare/self-managing-vs-hiring-a-property-manager/): what an owner takes on personally, priced against $137 per month, with eviction hard costs of about $448 to $450 in Franklin County.
- [RL Property Management vs. a typical Columbus property manager](/compare/rlpm-vs-typical-columbus-property-manager/): fee model, leasing fee, renewal fee, maintenance markup, approval limits, inspections, reporting, eviction handling, and published KPIs, side by side.

## Why is the comparison column called "market standard" instead of a company name?

Because a fee schedule is verifiable and a rival's internal practice is not. The market-standard Columbus model, 8 to 10 percent of monthly rent plus a leasing fee equal to one month's rent, is published widely enough to compare against honestly. Anything narrower would mean putting words in a named company's mouth, so items that differ by company read "varies."

## Where does a flat fee lose to a percentage?

At low rents in a year with no lease signing. On the Standard plan, a steady-state year runs $2,019 including the $250 renewal fee and the $125 annual accounting and technology fee. A manager charging 8 percent of $1,200 rent bills $1,152 in the same year. That comparison is published on the flat-fee page rather than hidden.

Comparisons above reflect RL Property Management pricing as of August 2026 and the market-standard model described in the company fact base. Terms are governed by the signed Property Management Agreement.
