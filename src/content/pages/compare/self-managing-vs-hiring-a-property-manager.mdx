## What does hiring a property manager cost compared with self-managing?

Self-managing costs $0 in management fees. Hiring RL Property Management on the Standard plan costs $137 per unit per month, or $1,769 in year one including the $125 annual accounting and technology fee, with no leasing fee when a resident signs. The comparison is not fee against fee. It is fee against the owner's time, pricing power, and legal exposure.

The fee side is fixed and knowable. The self-managing side is variable: a longer vacancy, an unscreened applicant, or a repair sourced under pressure can cost more in one event than a year of management fees.

## What happens when a tenancy goes wrong?

3.5 percent of RL Property Management tenancies escalate to eviction, litigation, or significant dispute. On a single self-managed property, that percentage is not a rate but a coin flip across the years an owner holds the asset. The cost of the bad outcome, rather than its frequency, is what a management fee buys down.

An eviction for possession only in Franklin County runs about $448 to $450 in hard costs as of August 2026: a $149 filing fee under the court cost schedule effective August 1, 2026, roughly $100 in attorney cost, and a $199 process fee. Filing with damages raises the court fee to $186. The process runs three steps and about six weeks, handled by local eviction attorneys. Lost rent, legal missteps, and turn costs sit on top of the hard costs.

## Which owners should keep self-managing?

Owners who live near the property, enjoy the work, have a vendor bench, and hold one or two units. Self-management is a real strategy, not a mistake. It stops working when the owner moves away, when unit count outgrows evenings and weekends, or when a tenancy escalates and the owner is learning Ohio eviction procedure while rent stops arriving.

RL Property Management declines some property types outright, including OSU-area campus rentals, rooms rented by the bedroom, and properties that are not yet rent-ready. An owner holding those keeps self-managing by default.
