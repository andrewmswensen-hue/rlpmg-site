## What is it like to work at RL Property Management?

RL Property Management is a {company.scale.employees}-person team managing approximately 750 rental units from one office in {company.address.city}, Ohio. The company manages a deliberately limited set of properties and neighborhoods rather than chasing volume, builds long relationships with investors, and runs on six published [core values](/core-values/). Systems are designed so a new hire can be effective quickly, and the work is measured on a public [KPI scorecard](/key-performance-indicators/).

## Which roles does RL Property Management hire for?

Roles fall into five groups: leasing (showing specialists, leasing leads), client and resident service, vacant-unit and maintenance coordination, maintenance technicians (RL Property Management employs its own rather than subcontracting), and office functions such as people operations and marketing. The [team page](/team/) lists current titles by name.

## How do I see open positions?

Current openings are posted on <a href="https://www.indeed.com/cmp/Rl-Property-Management-Group,-Inc./jobs" rel="noopener">RL Property Management's Indeed page</a>. Applications go through Indeed. Questions about a role can go to {company.contact.general.email}.
