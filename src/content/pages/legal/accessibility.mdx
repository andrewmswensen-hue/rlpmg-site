## What accessibility standard does rlpmg.com follow?

WCAG 2.2 Level AA, the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines published by the World Wide Web Consortium. The site is built and reviewed against that standard rather than certified by an outside auditor, and gaps found in review are logged and fixed. This statement is current as of August 2026. Accessibility is treated as a maintenance task on rlpmg.com, not a one-time project.

## What does the site do to meet the standard?

Seven things, applied across the site:

<ul>
  <li><strong>Semantic HTML.</strong> Headings run in order, lists are real lists, tables carry headers, and landmarks (header, nav, main, footer) let a screen reader jump between regions.</li>
  <li><strong>Keyboard navigation.</strong> Links, form fields, buttons, and calculators are reachable and operable by keyboard alone, with a skip link to the main content.</li>
  <li><strong>Visible focus.</strong> The focused element carries a visible outline that meets the WCAG 2.2 focus appearance requirement, so keyboard position is easy to see.</li>
  <li><strong>Contrast of at least 4.5 to 1.</strong> Body text, link text, and form labels meet the Level AA contrast ratio against their background.</li>
  <li><strong>Reduced motion.</strong> The site honors the operating system reduced-motion preference and stops non-essential movement when that preference is set.</li>
  <li><strong>Text alternatives.</strong> Meaningful images carry alt text describing what matters about them, and decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.</li>
  <li><strong>Facts in text.</strong> Prices, phone numbers, dates, KPI figures, and legal statements are selectable text on the page. Nothing load-bearing is locked inside an image, a PDF, or a script that has to run before the fact appears.</li>
</ul>

Pages render and forms submit with JavaScript turned off. A visitor using an older browser, a text browser, or an assistive tool that struggles with scripts still reaches the content and can still send a request.

## What are the known limitations?

Four embedded services, each operated by another company under its own accessibility program:

<ul>
  <li><strong>RentEngine</strong> powers the rental listings, showing scheduling, and the online application.</li>
  <li><strong>Calendly</strong> powers consultation scheduling for prospective owners.</li>
  <li><strong>Buildium</strong> powers the owner portal and the resident portal, including statements and online rent payment.</li>
  <li><strong>Property Meld</strong> powers maintenance requests.</li>
</ul>

Those tools are not built by RL Property Management, and their conformance is set by their vendors. Any task inside one of them can be completed instead by phone or email with staff: viewing a listing, scheduling a showing, submitting an application, making a rent payment arrangement, or reporting a maintenance issue.

Migrated blog articles published before this site was rebuilt may carry older images with thin alt text or embedded tables that read awkwardly. Those are corrected as they are found. Reporting one speeds that up.

## How do I report an accessibility barrier?

Email info@rlpmg.com with the word Accessibility in the subject line, or call 614.725.3059. Mail reaches RL Property Management, 750 Cross Pointe Rd, STE B, Gahanna, OH 43230. Include the page address, the browser or assistive technology in use, and what happened. The response target is 5 business days. If a fix takes longer, staff will handle the task directly so the request is not held up by the website.

## Can I request a reasonable accommodation in the rental process?

Yes. Federal fair housing law, Ohio law, and the Columbus city code require reasonable accommodations in rules, policies, practices, or services, and reasonable modifications to a unit, when needed for a person with a disability to use and enjoy a home. Requests are accepted at any stage: while touring, while applying, at lease signing, or during a tenancy. Email info@rlpmg.com or call 614.725.3059.

Common requests include an accessible tour arrangement, help completing an application, an alternate format for lease documents, an assistance animal exception to a pet policy, and a physical modification such as a grab bar or a ramp. Requests are evaluated individually and promptly. A request does not change how an application is scored against the published [application criteria](/residents/application-criteria/), and retaliation for making one is prohibited.
