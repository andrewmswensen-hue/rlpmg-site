## Who does this privacy policy cover?

This policy covers rlpmg.com and the forms on it. RL Property Management Group, Inc. is a residential property management company and licensed Ohio real estate brokerage (file REC.2015003677) at 750 Cross Pointe Rd, STE B, Gahanna, OH 43230. The policy applies to property owners, rental applicants, residents, vendors, and general visitors. Separate agreements govern signed clients and residents. This policy is effective August 2026.

## What information do the website forms collect?

The forms on rlpmg.com collect what a person types: name, email address, phone number, property address, and any message or free-text detail. Each submission also carries technical context gathered by the page: the URL of the page holding the form, the referring website, UTM parameters from the link that brought the visitor in, and an AI-assistant referral tag when the browser reports that the visit came from an assistant such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini.

The AI-assistant referral tag records the source of a visit. It records no conversation text and no account identity from the assistant.

Forms also carry a hidden field that is left blank by people and filled by automated scripts. A filled field marks the submission as spam. Nothing about a human visitor is stored in that field.

## Where do form submissions go?

Submissions are relayed by email to LeadSimple, the customer relationship management system RL Property Management uses to track owner inquiries, leasing questions, and service requests. Staff read the submission and respond. The relay passes through Cloudflare, which hosts rlpmg.com and processes the request in transit. {/* LEGAL REVIEW: confirm whether a data processing addendum with LeadSimple and Cloudflare should be referenced here. */}

Information submitted through a form is used to answer the request, to prepare a proposal or a rental application, and to keep a record of the contact. It is not sold, rented, or traded. It is not shared with advertisers.

Information is shared outside RL Property Management in four situations: with vendors performing work on a property, with the software services listed further down, with attorneys or courts where a lease or management matter requires it, and where Ohio or federal law requires disclosure.

## What analytics does rlpmg.com use?

Two: Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity. Google Analytics 4 counts page views, sessions, traffic sources, and which pages lead to a form submission. Microsoft Clarity records aggregated interaction data such as scroll depth and click position so page layout problems can be found. Both are loaded without blocking the page, and both operate under their own privacy terms as separate companies.

Neither tool receives the contents of a form field. Analytics measurement is about page behavior, not about the text a person types.

## What cookies does the site set?

rlpmg.com sets no advertising cookies and runs no retargeting pixels as of August 2026. Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity set their own cookies or similar browser identifiers to tell one visit from another. The forms use browser session storage to hold the first referring site and the AI-assistant tag for the length of a single visit, so attribution survives a click from one page to the next.

Cookies can be blocked or cleared in the browser settings. Pages and forms on rlpmg.com work with cookies blocked and with JavaScript turned off.

## Which third-party services have their own privacy policies?

Four services used alongside rlpmg.com are operated by other companies, and each handles data under its own policy rather than this one:

<ul>
  <li><strong>Buildium</strong> (rlp.managebuilding.com) runs the owner portal and the resident portal, including statements, ledgers, and online rent payment.</li>
  <li><strong>Property Meld</strong> (app.propertymeld.com) runs maintenance requests and the communication around a repair.</li>
  <li><strong>RentEngine</strong> (rentengine.io) runs the rental listings, showing scheduling, and the online rental application.</li>
  <li><strong>Calendly</strong> (calendly.com) runs consultation scheduling for prospective owners.</li>
</ul>

Reading a listing or booking a call on one of those services puts the information into that company's systems under that company's terms. Payment card and bank account details entered into the Buildium portal are handled by Buildium and its payment processor, not stored on rlpmg.com.

## How is applicant and resident information handled?

Rental application data is collected through RentEngine and the screening providers connected to it, not through rlpmg.com forms. Screening covers credit history, criminal history, rental history with landlord references, and income verified by pay stub or bank statement. Applications are evaluated by staff against the published [application criteria](/residents/application-criteria/) under fair housing law. {/* LEGAL REVIEW: confirm the FCRA adverse-action notice language and the screening vendor name before publication. */}

## How do I access, correct, or delete my information?

Email info@rlpmg.com, call 614.725.3059, or write to RL Property Management, 750 Cross Pointe Rd, STE B, Gahanna, OH 43230. Include the email address or phone number used so the record can be found. Requests are answered by staff. Records held under an active lease, an active management agreement, or a legal, accounting, or tax obligation are retained until that obligation ends, and the request is answered with that explanation. {/* LEGAL REVIEW: Ohio has no comprehensive consumer privacy statute as of August 2026; counsel should confirm whether out-of-state residents with statutory rights are addressed here. */}

## How is information protected?

Access to LeadSimple, Buildium, Property Meld, and RentEngine is limited to staff whose work requires it, and each service is reached over an encrypted connection. rlpmg.com is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is secure against each possible threat, and no assurance of absolute security is offered here. Suspected unauthorized access should be reported to info@rlpmg.com.

## How are changes to this policy handled?

A revised policy is posted on this page with a new effective date, and the date at the foot of the page changes with it. Material changes affecting owners or residents under an active agreement are communicated by email in addition to the posting. This version is effective August 2026.

RL Property Management does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 through rlpmg.com. A parent or guardian who believes a child submitted information should contact info@rlpmg.com so the record can be removed.
