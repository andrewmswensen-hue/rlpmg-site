## Is the content on rlpmg.com legal, tax, or investment advice?

No. Pages about Ohio landlord-tenant law, fair housing, eviction procedure, Columbus ordinances, rental pricing, and investment returns are written for general context and carry the date they were verified. Laws change, court practice varies, and a specific property has specific facts. Decisions about a lease, an eviction, a tax position, or an acquisition should be made with an attorney, an accountant, or a licensed advisor who knows the situation.

Nothing on rlpmg.com creates a brokerage, agency, fiduciary, or client relationship. That relationship begins when a Property Management Agreement is signed.

## What governs pricing and the services provided?

The signed Property Management Agreement. Published monthly fees, plan features, add-on charges, and policies on rlpmg.com describe the current published offering and carry a date. The agreement between an owner and RL Property Management sets the terms that apply to that owner and controls where the two differ. Published pricing is subject to change, and a change on the site does not by itself change a signed agreement.

Resident obligations run through the lease and its addenda, which control over any summary published on the site.

## Are the calculators and estimates warranted?

No warranty is offered on them. The rent versus sell calculator, the self-management cost calculator, rent ranges, turn cost ranges, eviction cost figures, and market data on rlpmg.com produce illustrations from the inputs a visitor supplies and from general assumptions about the Columbus, Ohio market. Output is not an appraisal, a broker price opinion, a quote, or a projection of a specific result.

Site content is provided as is. Figures are verified on the dates shown, and errors, omissions, and stale numbers are possible. Reporting one to info@rlpmg.com is welcome and gets a correction.
