## Who does this text message policy cover?

Three groups: property owners who give a mobile number to RL Property Management, rental applicants who provide one while touring or applying, and residents under a lease. The policy covers text messages sent by RL Property Management staff and by the systems the company uses to run leasing, maintenance, and rent collection. It is current as of August 2026.

Consent is given by entering a mobile number on a website form, a rental application, a lease, or a management agreement where the text-message option is marked, or by texting RL Property Management first.

## What kinds of messages are sent?

Five kinds, all tied to a property or a tenancy:

<ul>
  <li><strong>Leasing follow-ups.</strong> Answers to an inquiry about a home, application status, and next steps.</li>
  <li><strong>Showing confirmations.</strong> Confirmation of a scheduled tour, access instructions, and reminders before the appointment.</li>
  <li><strong>Maintenance updates.</strong> Technician scheduling, arrival windows, status changes, and confirmation that a repair is closed.</li>
  <li><strong>Rent reminders.</strong> Due date reminders, payment confirmations, and notices about a balance.</li>
  <li><strong>Time-sensitive notices.</strong> Utility shutoffs, weather-related property instructions, inspection scheduling, and lease renewal deadlines.</li>
</ul>

Marketing blasts unrelated to a property or a tenancy are not part of this program.

## How often will messages arrive?

Frequency varies. An active rental search or an open maintenance issue can produce several messages in a week, while a quiet tenancy may produce none for months. No fixed message count is promised, and no minimum is sent. Volume tracks what is happening with the property, the application, or the repair.

## Do message and data rates apply?

They may. RL Property Management charges nothing to send or receive these texts. The mobile carrier sets message and data rates under the recipient's plan, and those charges apply the same way they do for any other text. Anyone unsure about a plan should check with the carrier before opting in.

## How do I opt out or get help?

Reply STOP to any message from RL Property Management. One confirmation is sent and messages to that number stop. Reply HELP to any message to get contact information for the company. Opting out can also be handled by calling 614.725.3059 or emailing info@rlpmg.com, and it can be reversed by replying START or asking staff to re-enable texts.

Opting out of texts does not opt a person out of required legal notices. Notices a lease or Ohio law requires in writing continue by mail, email, or hand delivery, as the lease specifies.

## Is consent required to rent a home or hire RL Property Management?

No. Text consent is optional and is not a condition of applying for a rental, signing a lease, or entering a Property Management Agreement. Anyone who prefers phone or email is served the same way. Declining texts does not affect an application, a lease, a maintenance request, or the terms offered to an owner.

## Are carriers responsible for delayed or undelivered messages?

No. Mobile carriers do not warrant that a message will be delivered or that it will arrive on time, and carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. RL Property Management is not liable for a delay or failure in the carrier network either. Anything urgent should go to the phone line at 614.725.3059, which reaches the 24/7 emergency maintenance service outside office hours.

## How is my mobile number handled?

The number is stored in the RL Property Management CRM, LeadSimple, and in the systems used for leasing, maintenance, and accounting. It is used to reach the person about a property, an application, a tenancy, or a request. Mobile numbers and consent status are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own marketing. Full detail is in the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/).

Questions about this policy go to info@rlpmg.com or 614.725.3059, or to RL Property Management, 750 Cross Pointe Rd, STE B, Gahanna, OH 43230.
