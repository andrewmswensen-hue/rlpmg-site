## What are the steps to onboard a rental property with RL Property Management?

Onboarding runs in eight steps, from rent evaluation to the first direct deposit. Two of the eight cost money up front: the optional $300 pre-inspection, and the $300 onboarding fee on a vacant unit. Placement itself is free, because RL Property Management charges a $0 leasing fee rather than a fee equal to one month's rent.

<ol class="rl-steps not-prose">
  <li><strong>Rent evaluation and consultation.</strong> A rent evaluation puts a number on what the property should rent for in the Columbus, Ohio market. The consultation covers property type, condition, and which plan fits the owner.</li>
  <li><strong>Optional $300 pre-inspection.</strong> RL Property Management walks the property and produces a punch list. The $300 is credited against the onboarding fee once an agreement is signed.</li>
  <li><strong>Sign the Property Management Agreement and choose a plan.</strong> Passive at $117, Standard at $137, or Premium at $184 per unit per month.</li>
  <li><strong>Fund onboarding.</strong> A $300 onboarding fee on a vacant unit, plus a maintenance reserve of $1,500, $750, or $350 depending on plan. Utilities stay on.</li>
  <li><strong>Photograph and list on 45+ platforms.</strong> Zillow, Trulia, Apartments.com, and rlpmg.com among them. Craigslist is excluded.</li>
  <li><strong>Screen applicants.</strong> Income at 3x rent, no evictions in 5 years, credit and criminal history, rental history and landlord references.</li>
  <li><strong>Move-in inspection and lease signing.</strong> Written condition documentation, then lease signing and collection of a deposit of 1 to 2 months' rent depending on credit.</li>
  <li><strong>First monthly statement and direct deposit.</strong> Net income by direct deposit, statement by email, figures visible in the owner portal.</li>
</ol>

## What happens before the Property Management Agreement is signed?

Two things: a rent evaluation and, if the owner wants one, a $300 pre-inspection. The rent evaluation sets the asking rent. The pre-inspection produces a written punch list of repairs standing between the property and a listing, which matters because RL Property Management declines renovation projects on properties that are not yet rent-ready.

The $300 pre-inspection fee is credited against the $300 onboarding fee once the agreement is signed, so an owner who proceeds pays $300 once rather than twice. An owner who decides not to proceed keeps the punch list.

## What does it cost to start, and what has to be funded up front?

Three line items, as of August 2026. A $300 onboarding fee applies to a vacant unit. A maintenance reserve is funded per unit at the plan's repair approval limit: $1,500 on Passive, $750 on Standard, $350 on Premium. Listing photography is billed at vendor cost plus markup, typically $250 to $450, effective October 1, 2026.

Existing professional photos already on file are used at no charge, which takes photography to $0 for an owner transferring a recently marketed property. The maintenance reserve is not a fee: the money stays in the owner's account and funds repairs.

## How does the property get listed and leased?

The listing goes out to 45+ rental platforms, including Zillow, Trulia, Apartments.com, and rlpmg.com. Craigslist is excluded. Showings, applications, and screening run through RL Property Management, and the owner pays no leasing fee when a resident signs. Quality of placement is prioritized over speed of placement.

## Who gets approved to rent the property?

Applicants meet a published standard: gross income at 3x the monthly rent, no evictions in the past 5 years, no violent criminal convictions, and no serious or recurring credit issues. Screening also covers rental history with landlord references and income verification by pay stub or bank statement. The security deposit is 1 to 2 months' rent depending on credit.

Publishing the standard has a second effect. Applicants who do not meet it can see so before paying an application fee, which keeps the applicant pool closer to the owner's actual pool.

## How long does onboarding take?

Average lease-up is 3 to 4 weeks once the property is rent-ready, as of August 2026. No lease timeline is promised, because a promised date pushes toward a fast placement rather than a good one. If a property stays vacant longer than 60 days, the monthly management fee on that unit is reimbursed.

A property that is not yet rent-ready starts the clock later. Repairs on the pre-inspection punch list happen before the listing goes live, and their duration depends on scope and vendor availability.

## When does the first payment reach the owner?

After the first full month of collected rent. Net income goes out by direct deposit and the monthly statement arrives by email, with the same figures visible in the online owner portal.
