## Where does RL Property Management advertise a Columbus rental?

Each listing syndicates to {leasing.platforms.label}, including {leasing.platforms.examples.join(', ')}. Craigslist is excluded, because the applicant quality that comes back from it does not justify the volume. Listings carry photos, a written description, and self-scheduled showing availability, and applications are taken online rather than on paper.

Available homes are published on a single RL Property Management listings feed, and applications are submitted online from the listing itself. Cutting the gap between a renter finding the listing and starting an application is what protects the {dom}-day median: a prospect who has to email for an application form is a prospect who keeps scrolling.

Breadth matters less than where renter traffic actually starts in the Columbus, Ohio market, which is why the major portals carry the listing on day one rather than after a week on the company site alone.

## How long does it take to lease a rent-ready unit?

Average lease-up runs {leasing.averageLeaseUpWeeks.min} to {leasing.averageLeaseUpWeeks.max} weeks once the unit is rent-ready, and median days on market across the RL Property Management portfolio was {dom} days as of {kpis.asOfLabel}. {leasing.averageLeaseUpWeeks.note} Occupancy sat at {kpis.metrics.find((m) => m.id === 'occupancy').value} percent over the same period.

Two variables move that number more than marketing does: the asking rent and the condition of the unit. A Columbus property priced at market with a finished turn leases inside the range; a property priced above market collects showings and no applications.

## What happens if the property sits vacant?

{pricing.vacancyGuarantee} The reimbursement is what puts RL Property Management on the same side of the table as the owner during a lease-up: a vacant unit costs the manager money too, starting on day 61.

Utilities stay active during vacancy for turnover work, repairs, and winter protection. Median time to turn a unit between residents was {kpis.metrics.find((m) => m.id === 'time-to-turn').value} days as of {kpis.asOfLabel}, and the turn happens before the listing goes live rather than during showings.

## Who pays for listing photography?

The owner. {photo.policy} The policy takes effect {monthYear(photo.effectiveDate)}. {photo.grandfather}

Professional photos are the single highest-leverage line in a listing budget. A renter scrolling Zillow in the Columbus market decides in about a second, and phone photos of a dim living room lose that second to the listing above.

## How does RL Property Management pick between applicants?

{leasing.placementPhilosophy} Applicants are measured against published standards: income of {leasing.qualification.incomeMultiple} times the monthly rent, no violent criminal convictions, no eviction filed within the past {leasing.qualification.noEvictionsWithinYears} years, and no serious or recurring credit issues. The full screening process is on the tenant screening page.

A renewal costs {usd(pricing.fees.leaseRenewalFee)}.
