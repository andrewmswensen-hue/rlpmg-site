## How often does RL Property Management inspect a rental?

{maintenance.inspections.frequency}, four times a year, for each managed property across the Columbus, Ohio metro. Two more inspections bracket each tenancy: a move-in ready inspection before a resident takes possession, and a move-out inspection that also prepares the turn scope. All three inspection types are included on the Passive, Standard, and Premium plans as of August 2026.

Four visits a year is more than a Columbus owner gets from a manager who inspects annually, and the reason is arithmetic rather than diligence theater. A slow leak found in October costs a trap and an hour of labor; the same leak found the following August costs subfloor.

## What does a quarterly inspection cover?

Five areas: smoke detectors, appliances, resident responsibilities under the lease, liabilities, and overall condition of the property. The inspector walks the unit against that list rather than forming a general impression, which is what makes one quarter comparable to the next and lets a trend show up before it becomes a repair.

## What inspections happen when a resident moves in or out?

A move-in ready inspection confirms the unit is clean, safe, and functional before possession transfers, and it establishes the documented baseline condition. A move-out inspection records condition against that baseline and produces the turn scope, which is the work list that gets the unit back to rent-ready. Both are included on all three plans.

Median time to turn a unit between residents was {turnDays} days as of {kpis.asOfLabel}. That figure depends directly on the move-out inspection: a scope written the day the keys come back gets technicians dispatched, while a scope written a week later adds a week of vacancy.

## Does an inspection cost anything beyond the monthly fee?

No. Quarterly, move-in, and move-out inspections are covered by the flat monthly fee of {usd(pricing.plans[0].monthlyFee)}, {usd(pricing.plans[1].monthlyFee)}, or {usd(pricing.plans[2].monthlyFee)} per unit as of {pricing.effectiveLabel}. Repairs identified during an inspection are billed separately: {usd(pricing.fees.inHouseMaintenance.hourlyRate)} per hour plus a {usd(pricing.fees.inHouseMaintenance.tripCharge)} trip charge, plus materials.

Whether a repair found during an inspection needs owner sign-off depends on the plan. Passive authorizes up to {usd(pricing.plans[0].maintenanceApprovalLimit)} per item, Standard up to {usd(pricing.plans[1].maintenanceApprovalLimit)}, Premium up to {usd(pricing.plans[2].maintenanceApprovalLimit)}.

## What is the pre-inspection before a management agreement?

A {usd(pricing.fees.preInspection.amount)} inspection performed before signing, which produces a punch list of what stands between the property and a rental listing. {pricing.fees.preInspection.note} For a vacant unit, the onboarding fee is {usd(pricing.fees.onboardingFeeVacantUnit)}, so the pre-inspection effectively costs nothing once the owner proceeds.

The pre-inspection exists because RL Property Management declines renovation projects on properties that are not yet rent-ready. An owner in Columbus, Ohio gets a written answer on where the property stands before either side commits.

## How do inspections protect an owner's deposit position?

Documented condition is what makes a deposit deduction defensible. The move-in ready inspection sets the baseline, quarterly inspections record how the property is being kept, and the move-out inspection records the difference. Security deposits run {leasing.securityDeposit}, and approved pet households carry {usd(leasing.petDamageCoverage.amount)} of pet damage coverage funded by a monthly pet administration fee paid by the resident.

## What happens with smoke and carbon monoxide detectors?

{maintenance.detectors} Detector checks run at each quarterly visit rather than once a year, and age-based replacement removes the guesswork about a unit that has been quietly out of code since a prior owner installed the devices.

Utilities stay active during vacancy for turnover work, repairs, and winter protection, so an inspection on an empty Columbus property in January still has heat, water, and light to work with.

An owner deciding whether a property is ready for management can start with the {usd(pricing.fees.preInspection.amount)} pre-inspection and get the punch list before signing anything.
