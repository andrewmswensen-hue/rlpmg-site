## When does a repair need owner approval?

When the cost passes the plan's approval limit. Below the limit, RL Property Management authorizes the repair and the resident gets a fix without waiting on a phone call. Above it, the owner decides. The limit is the main lever separating the three plans, and it moves in the opposite direction from the monthly fee: the lowest fee carries the highest approval authority.

## How fast does a repair get done?

Routine repairs complete in {maintenance.routineCompletionWeeks.min} to {maintenance.routineCompletionWeeks.max} weeks, and the median repair across the RL Property Management portfolio ran {repairDays} days as of {kpis.asOfLabel}. Emergencies are dispatched on receipt rather than scheduled. Median time to turn a unit between residents was {turnDays} days over the same period.

Requests come in through a dedicated maintenance request system rather than by voicemail, so the clock starts when the resident submits rather than when someone returns a call. The same record follows the job through dispatch, completion, and the charge that lands on the owner statement.

Speed depends on parts, trade availability, and resident access to the unit. A furnace part on backorder in a Columbus, Ohio winter is outside anyone's control, and RL Property Management publishes the median rather than promising a ceiling.

## What happens at 2 a.m. or on a holiday?

A {maintenance.emergencyLine} answers, triages, and dispatches. Emergency coverage is included on all three plans at no separate charge. The call center exists so a burst supply line in Gahanna at midnight becomes a dispatched technician rather than a voicemail an owner finds on Monday.

Utilities stay active during vacancy for turnover work, repairs, and winter protection, which is what keeps an empty Columbus property from freezing between residents.

## Can an owner use their own contractor?

On the Premium plan, an owner's preferred vendors can be used. Owner-handled repairs on vacant units are available on the Standard and Premium plans. No RL Property Management plan permits owner-handled repairs on an occupied unit, because the resident relationship, the liability, and the response clock sit with the manager once a lease is signed.

Home warranties are also declined across all three plans. Warranty claim approval inserts a third party between the resident and the repair, and the delay lands on the resident.

## Why is a maintenance reserve held on each unit?

Each unit carries a reserve equal to the plan's approval limit: {usd(pricing.plans[2].maintenanceReserve)}, {usd(pricing.plans[1].maintenanceReserve)}, or {usd(pricing.plans[0].maintenanceReserve)}. The reserve funds an approved repair the day it is authorized instead of waiting on a transfer from the owner. Beyond that, RL Property Management recommends owners hold {pricing.reserves.recommendedOwnerReserve} in their own reserve for larger capital items.

Owners comparing plan approval limits against their own tolerance for repair calls can see the full fee schedule on the pricing page.
