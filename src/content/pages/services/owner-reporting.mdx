  m.unit === 'USD/month' ? `${usd(m.value)} per month` : m.unit === '%' ? `${m.value} percent` : `${m.value} ${m.unit}`;

## What reporting does an RL Property Management owner receive?

Four things, on all three plans: a monthly statement by email, direct deposit of net income to the owner's bank account, financial recordkeeping held on the owner's behalf, and access to an online owner portal. No plan upgrade unlocks better reporting. An owner on Passive at {usd(pricing.plans[0].monthlyFee)} per unit sees the same ledger as an owner on Premium at {usd(pricing.plans[2].monthlyFee)}.

Reporting is the part of property management an owner touches most, and it is where a manager either earns trust or loses it. RL Property Management publishes the fee schedule, the operating metrics, and the ledger rather than summarizing them.

## When does the statement arrive and when does the money land?

The statement is emailed monthly and net income is deposited directly for the same period. The specific calendar day each month that statements go out and deposits initiate is [SOURCE NEEDED].

What comes out before the deposit is published: the flat monthly management fee, the unit's maintenance reserve, repairs incurred that month, and any other fees named in the signed Property Management Agreement. Reserves are held at {usd(pricing.plans[2].maintenanceReserve)}, {usd(pricing.plans[1].maintenanceReserve)}, or {usd(pricing.plans[0].maintenanceReserve)} per unit depending on plan.

## What does the owner portal do?

The owner portal runs at rlp.managebuilding.com and shows the ledger for each managed unit: rent posted, repairs charged, fees applied, and deposits made. Portal access is included on the Passive, Standard, and Premium plans at no separate charge. Residents use a separate portal for rent payment and maintenance requests, which keeps the two ledgers in sync without manual entry.

An owner checking whether the 3rd-of-the-month rent payment posted does not need to call the office in Gahanna, Ohio to find out.

## What is the annual accounting and technology fee?

{usd(pricing.fees.annualAccountingAndTechnologyFee)} per unit, charged once a year as of {pricing.effectiveLabel}. The fee covers accounting work and the technology behind statements, the portal, and recordkeeping. Keeping it annual rather than folding it into the monthly fee is deliberate: the monthly number an owner compares against other Columbus, Ohio managers stays the number actually charged each month.

Full fee disclosure is the point. Onboarding on a vacant unit is {usd(pricing.fees.onboardingFeeVacantUnit)}, lease renewal is {usd(pricing.fees.leaseRenewalFee)}, and the leasing fee is {usd(pricing.fees.leasingFee)}.

## How does a repair show up on the statement?

As a line item against the unit, with labor billed at {usd(pricing.fees.inHouseMaintenance.hourlyRate)} per hour plus a {usd(pricing.fees.inHouseMaintenance.tripCharge)} trip charge and materials at cost. Third-party vendor work carries a {pricing.fees.vendorProjectManagement.percent} percent project management fee on vendor cost up to {usd(pricing.fees.vendorProjectManagement.appliesToVendorCostUpTo)}, capped at {usd(pricing.fees.vendorProjectManagement.maxPerProject)} per project. Turn work is charged on a separate sliding scale starting at {pricing.fees.turnProjectManagement[0].percent} percent.

Whether a repair reaches the statement without a prior phone call depends on the plan approval limit: {usd(pricing.plans[0].maintenanceApprovalLimit)} on Passive, {usd(pricing.plans[1].maintenanceApprovalLimit)} on Standard, {usd(pricing.plans[2].maintenanceApprovalLimit)} on Premium. An owner who wants fewer surprises on the statement picks the lower limit and accepts the higher monthly fee.

## What operating numbers does RL Property Management publish?

{kpis.metrics.length} metrics, updated as of {kpis.asOfLabel}, covering leasing speed, turn speed, retention, occupancy, rent level, repair speed, collections, and resident satisfaction. Few property management companies in the Columbus market publish operating data at all, which makes the scorecard the fastest way for an owner to compare RL Property Management against a manager who offers adjectives.
