## Who gets approved to live in the property?

Applicants who document income of {leasing.qualification.incomeMultiple} times the monthly rent, carry no violent criminal convictions, have no eviction filed within the past {leasing.qualification.noEvictionsWithinYears} years, and show no serious or recurring credit issues. Deposits run {leasing.securityDeposit}, and approved pet households carry {usd(leasing.petDamageCoverage.amount)} of pet damage coverage funded by the resident. Details are on [tenant screening in Columbus, Ohio](/services/tenant-screening/).

## How does the rent get collected and reach the owner?

Residents pay through an online portal, and {kpis.metrics.find((m) => m.id === 'collected-by-5th').value} percent of rent was collected by the 5th of the month as of {kpis.asOfLabel}. Net income is deposited directly to the owner's bank account with a statement emailed the same period. The full breakdown of what comes out before the deposit is on [rent collection and owner payouts](/services/rent-collection/).

## Who fixes things when they break?

An in-house team of {company.scale.maintenanceTechnicians} technicians, billed at {usd(pricing.fees.inHouseMaintenance.hourlyRate)} per hour plus a {usd(pricing.fees.inHouseMaintenance.tripCharge)} trip charge and materials. Repairs at or under the plan approval limit of {usd(pricing.plans[0].maintenanceApprovalLimit)}, {usd(pricing.plans[1].maintenanceApprovalLimit)}, or {usd(pricing.plans[2].maintenanceApprovalLimit)} proceed without an owner call. Routine work completes in {maintenance.routineCompletionWeeks.min} to {maintenance.routineCompletionWeeks.max} weeks. See [rental property maintenance](/services/maintenance/).

## How often does anyone look at the property?

Quarterly, checking smoke detectors, appliances, resident responsibilities, liabilities, and overall condition, plus a move-in ready inspection and a move-out inspection that prepares the turn scope. Smoke and CO detectors are replaced at 10 years or older. Inspection detail, including the {usd(pricing.fees.preInspection.amount)} pre-inspection before signing, is on [rental property inspections](/services/property-inspections/).

## How does a vacant unit get leased?

Listings go out to {leasing.platforms.label} including {leasing.platforms.examples.slice(0, 3).join(', ')}, and average lease-up runs {leasing.averageLeaseUpWeeks.min} to {leasing.averageLeaseUpWeeks.max} weeks once the unit is rent-ready. Median days on market was {kpis.metrics.find((m) => m.id === 'days-on-market').value} as of {kpis.asOfLabel}. {pricing.vacancyGuarantee} See [leasing and marketing for Columbus rentals](/services/leasing-and-marketing/).

## What happens when a tenancy fails?

Pay-and-stay arrangements come first. When those fail, local eviction attorneys file in {evictions.court}: about {usd(evictions.costs.totalPossessionOnlyApprox.min)} to {usd(evictions.costs.totalPossessionOnlyApprox.max)} for possession only and roughly {evictions.typicalDurationWeeks} weeks as of {monthYear(evictions.costs.asOf)}. {leasing.escalationRate.percent} percent of rentals reach that point. Cost and process detail sits on [evictions in Franklin County, Ohio](/services/evictions/), which is informational and not legal advice.

## What does an owner see month to month?

A statement by email, a direct deposit, and an online owner portal with the ledger for each unit, identical on all three plans. RL Property Management also publishes {kpis.metrics.length} operating metrics as of {kpis.asOfLabel}. The reporting stack, the {usd(pricing.fees.annualAccountingAndTechnologyFee)} annual fee, and the machine-readable data feeds are covered on [owner reporting and the owner portal](/services/owner-reporting/).
