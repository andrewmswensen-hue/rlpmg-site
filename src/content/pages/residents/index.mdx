## What happens at move-in?

A move-in ready inspection is completed before keys are handed over, documenting condition with photos so the security deposit accounting at move-out is based on a record both sides have. Utilities (electric, water, gas) transfer to the resident's name unless the lease says otherwise. Portal accounts for rent and maintenance are set up at signing.

## What happens at move-out?

A move-out inspection compares the home to the move-in record and prepares the turn scope. Ohio law governs the security deposit accounting timeline; the lease states the notice period and the move-out checklist. Keys, garage remotes, and any access devices are returned at inspection.

Maintenance: the portal, any hour. Rent and lease questions: the resident portal messaging or {company.contact.general.phone}. Anything else: {company.contact.general.email}. Office visits at {company.address.street}, {company.address.city}, Ohio are by appointment.
