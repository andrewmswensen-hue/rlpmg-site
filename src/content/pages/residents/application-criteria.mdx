## Why publish the criteria?

So that an applicant can judge fit before paying a fee, and so that each applicant is measured against the same written standard. RL Property Management applies the criteria consistently, in line with federal, Ohio, and Columbus fair housing law, which in Columbus includes protection based on source of income.
