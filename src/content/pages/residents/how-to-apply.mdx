## What are the steps to apply?

<ol>
  <li><strong>View the home.</strong> Drive by first, then schedule a showing from the listing or take a self-guided tour where offered.</li>
  <li><strong>Apply online.</strong> <a href={company.portals.apply} rel="noopener">Open the application</a>. Each adult 18 or older applies separately; the fee is $100 per person, with $50 refunded on approval and move-in.</li>
  <li><strong>Screening.</strong> Credit history, criminal history, rental history with landlord references, and income verified by pay stub or bank statement, against the published <a href="/residents/application-criteria/">criteria</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Lease signing and move-in costs.</strong> Sign electronically. Pay the security deposit ({leasing.securityDeposit}), the first full month's rent plus any proration, and the lease-signing administrative fee of 10 percent of one month's rent.</li>
</ol>

## What does RL Property Management check?

Four things, for each adult applicant: credit history, criminal history, rental history including references from current and prior landlords, and income, which must be at least {leasing.qualification.incomeMultiple} times the monthly rent and is verified by pay stubs or bank statements. Applicants with an eviction in the past {leasing.qualification.noEvictionsWithinYears} years or a violent criminal conviction are not approved. The same standards apply to each applicant for each home, consistent with fair housing law.

## How are applications prioritized?

First come, first served. The first complete application that meets the criteria is approved; later applicants for the same home are told promptly so they can apply elsewhere. A complete application includes all adults, all documents, and the fee.

## What happens after approval?

A lease is sent for electronic signature, move-in costs are collected, a move-in inspection documents the condition of the home, and the resident portal (free online rent payment) and maintenance portal (24/7) are set up. Keys are released at move-in.
