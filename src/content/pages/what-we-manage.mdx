## Which property types does RL Property Management accept?

RL Property Management accepts residential rentals that can be run without on-site staff: single-family homes, condos and townhomes in associations that allow rentals, two to four unit buildings, and multi-family properties up to roughly 100 units. The focus is class A and class B properties; class C properties are considered case by case. Properties must be rent-ready on the day management begins.

## Which property types does RL Property Management decline?

RL Property Management declines seven categories on all three plans. The list is published so an owner can rule the company in or out before a call. Campus rentals and by-the-bedroom leases are declined because they require a different operating model; home warranties are declined because third-party claim approval slows repairs for residents.

## Does RL Property Management work with properties inside an HOA?

Yes. RL Property Management manages rentals located inside a homeowners or condo association where the association permits rentals. The Standard and Premium plans include support for association requirements, such as registering residents and responding to violation notices. RL Property Management does not manage the association itself and does not take HOA or COA management contracts.

## What condition does a property need to be in?

A property needs to be rent-ready: clean, safe, with working mechanical systems, and with no open renovation work. Before a management agreement is signed, RL Property Management offers a pre-inspection for $300, credited against the onboarding fee once the agreement is signed. The inspection produces a punch list so an owner knows what, if anything, stands between the property and a listing.
