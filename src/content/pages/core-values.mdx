## What are RL Property Management's core values?

RL Property Management works from six values. Each one describes a behavior, not a slogan, and each is listed below with what it means in the day-to-day work of managing approximately 750 Columbus, Ohio rental units. The purpose behind them is plain: help property owners succeed with their real estate investments while treating residents and teammates well.

<dl>
  <dt><strong>Clear Communication</strong></dt>
  <dd>Each resident request, owner update, and internal process is communicated directly, so the message is effective and easy to understand the first time.</dd>
  <dt><strong>Do It In Realtime</strong></dt>
  <dd>Lease renewals are processed, maintenance requests are scheduled, and client updates are documented immediately, so there are no lost details and no unnecessary follow-ups.</dd>
  <dt><strong>Figure It Out and Get It Done</strong></dt>
  <dd>An urgent repair, a resident dispute, or an owner's financial report gets an owner on the team who finds a solution and sees the task through to completion.</dd>
  <dt><strong>A Place for Everything and Everything in Its Place</strong></dt>
  <dd>Each lease, invoice, and resident request is tracked and stored so nothing gets lost and the right information is available when it is needed.</dd>
  <dt><strong>We Lend a Hand</strong></dt>
  <dd>Helping owners succeed, responding quickly to residents, and supporting teammates without hesitation.</dd>
  <dt><strong>Respect Clients, Residents, and Each Other</strong></dt>
  <dd>A resident concern, an owner inquiry, or a team discussion gets active listening, a prompt response, and professional handling.</dd>
</dl>

## How do the values affect an owner's experience?

They show up as response times and documentation. Do It In Realtime is why maintenance requests are scheduled as they arrive and why the owner portal reflects activity the same day. Clear Communication is why the monthly statement and the published [pricing](/pricing/) are written to be understood without a call. The [KPI scorecard](/key-performance-indicators/) is the measurable result: median repair days, percent of rent collected by the 5th, and renewal rate, updated monthly.

## How do the values affect a resident's experience?

Residents are named in the values alongside owners because a property manager serves both. Respect Clients, Residents, and Each Other means a maintenance request or a lease question is answered with the same care as an owner inquiry, and the 24/7 emergency line exists so an urgent repair is handled when it happens.

The values are reviewed with each new hire and referenced in performance reviews, as of August 2026.
