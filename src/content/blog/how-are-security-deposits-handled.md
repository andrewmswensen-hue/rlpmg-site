We receive many questions about security deposits. In the video below we answer a few:
<ul data-rte-list="default">
 	<li>Who holds the deposit while the resident occupies the unit?
<ul data-rte-list="default">
 	<li>RL Property Management holds these funds in our Property Management Trust account as required by Ohio law.</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>How much is the security deposit?
<ul data-rte-list="default">
 	<li>RL Property Management typically charges one month's rent as a security deposit. If a rental application is on the fence of being approved for one reason or another, we may elect to charge two month's rent as a security deposit.</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>Who keeps the security deposit at the end of occupancy?
<ul data-rte-list="default">
 	<li>Based on a detailed comparison of the move-in condition and the move-out condition we withhold funds from the deposit to cover the following:
<ul data-rte-list="default">
 	<li>Tenant Damages (Purposeful, Accidental)</li>
 	<li>Abnormal Wear and Tear</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>Tenants cannot be charged for all "repairs". Normal "wear and tear" and functionality (door stops, rubbing doors, improper outlets, etc.) issues cannot be charged back to the resident.</li>
 	<li>We may also withhold funds from the deposit to cover unpaid rent, unpaid utility bills, unpaid fees, maintenance charges, etc.</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>What if the deposit is insufficient to cover damages?
<ul data-rte-list="default">
 	<li>RL Property Management sends a final "invoice" to past residents showing them any outstanding balance.</li>
 	<li>If unpaid, any outstanding balance would have to be collected through the legal system.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
