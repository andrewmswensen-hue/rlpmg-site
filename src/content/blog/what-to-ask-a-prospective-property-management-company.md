<p id="yui_3_17_2_1_1560882124445_480">Property owners and property managers (PM) each have many expectations of the other. For this reason, both parties should ask many questions of the other before working together. A PM is going to take care of many time consuming tasks over the years saving the owner hundreds of hours of effort. <strong>We would recommend all owners research prospective PM's using the following questions:</strong></p>
Updated: March 5, 2019
<ol data-rte-list="default">
 	<li>Is your brokerage licensed with the State of Ohio?</li>
 	<li>Does your brokerage provide services other than Property Management?</li>
 	<li>How many units do you manage?</li>
 	<li>Are your services an all-or-nothing approach or more of an a la carte approach?</li>
 	<li>In what ways could an owner be involved with the management of the property?</li>
 	<li>How often will the property be visited by your staff?</li>
 	<li>What will be checked at property visits?</li>
 	<li>What geographic area is your brokerage experienced in managing?</li>
 	<li>Where are prospective tenants looking for rentals and do you advertise there?</li>
 	<li>When they arise, how are maintenance needs handled?</li>
 	<li>When they arise, how are capital spending needs (long lived improvements) handled?</li>
 	<li>What is your process for handling lease violations other than non-payment of rent?</li>
 	<li>When will I, the owner be contacted?</li>
 	<li>Are your prospective tenant screening criteria the same across the board?</li>
 	<li>How many weeks of vacancy should I expect between tenants?</li>
 	<li>Can you provide me some examples of common repairs and their associated costs?</li>
 	<li>When was the last time your lease template was updated in a major way?</li>
 	<li>What kind of process refinement to better manage my property is taking place at your company?</li>
 	<li>Can I handle maintenance repairs while my units are occupied?</li>
 	<li>Do you support "Home Warranties" and if not why?</li>
 	<li>How do you minimize the chance of a bad outcome with a tenant (unpaid rent, lease violations, legal trouble, general strife)?</li>
 	<li>What "after hours" maintenance work could actually happen and who will the tenant be talking to after-hours?</li>
 	<li>Do you care how long I own the property?</li>
 	<li>My property is part of an association, who will deal with association issues?</li>
 	<li>What steps are taken to "renew" the tenants lease?</li>
 	<li>How do prospective tenants schedule showings at my units with your company?</li>
 	<li>Who determines what work needs done to the unit between tenants?</li>
 	<li>What is your occupancy rate now and on average?</li>
 	<li>Do I have access 24/7 to all invoices for expenses at my property?</li>
 	<li>Why might my unit not lease quickly? If leasing is slow, what are we going to do?</li>
 	<li>What percentage of your tenants receive a 3-day notice?</li>
 	<li>What is explicitly excluded from your services?</li>
 	<li>Other than your 2-3 main fees, what other expenses might you charge me?</li>
 	<li>As an owner, how often will I have to send you money?</li>
 	<li>How do you ensure carbon monoxide and smoke detectors are in place and working?</li>
 	<li>What is your pet policy?</li>
 	<li>How will utility transfers and billing be handled while the unit is vacant?</li>
 	<li>How do you check for lease violations and which lease violations do you check for?</li>
 	<li>How do you ensure the furnace filter gets replaced?</li>
</ol>
