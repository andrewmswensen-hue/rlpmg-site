<p class="">All of these assumptions are wrong.</p>

<ul data-rte-list="default">
 	<li>
<p class="">They won't declare bankruptcy while living at the property</p>

<ul data-rte-list="default">
 	<li>
<p class=""> And if they do, you can evict them</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They won't make partial payments</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They won't make partial online payments</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They understand your late fee policy</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will pay the water bill</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will report water leaks such as running toilets or under the sink</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will change furnace filters</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They don't have pets and never will</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They won't lie about assistance animals</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will obtain renter's insurance</p>

<ul data-rte-list="default">
 	<li>
<p class="">And won't cancel their policy after moving in</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will report any additional occupants who move in or turn 18</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will sign and return lease renewals</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will provide 30 days' notice to vacate</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will report fires that occur inside the property</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will lock doors and windows</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will maintain gas service on at the property</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will leave the heat on when they leave for vacation or move out</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They won't use the oven to heat your home</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They won't use multiple space heaters with extension cords to heat your home</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will allow you to view the property with reasonable notice</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They won't change the locks</p>

<ul data-rte-list="default">
 	<li>
<p class="">Or if they do, they will give you a copy of the new key</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They won't add locks to interior bedroom doors</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They won't paint the walls without asking</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will vacuum the carpets at any time</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will respond rationally to nominal rent increases</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They understand month-to-month tenancy</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They read things</p>

<ul data-rte-list="default">
 	<li>
<p class="">Or if they don't, at the very least they will read your lease before signing it</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They understand the concept of market rent</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They know how to submit maintenance requests and will do so</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will move out on or before the last day of their lease</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will turn in their keys upon move out</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will notify you in any way when they move out</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They understand the concept of a nonrefundable pet deposit/fee</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will provide a forwarding address for the security deposit</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will not try to take every possible advantage of simple mistakes or errors made by you</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They understand pro-rated rent</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They won't break up, get divorced, or lose their job</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will not die or commit suicide</p>

<ul data-rte-list="default">
 	<li>
<p class="">Or if they do, it won't be in your property</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">Their personal check can't bounce after 3 business days</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">Their online ACH payment can't bounce after 3 business days</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They understand the concept of "jointly and severally liable"</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They won't smoke inside the property</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They won't disable or steal smoke detectors</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They understand how to use common household appliances such as dishwashers</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They understand what a circuit breaker is and how to reset it</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They understand what a GFCI outlet is</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They are not physically or mentally disabled and will not become disabled</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will not modify the structure of your property</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will report sump pump backup battery failure alarms</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They understand how thermostats work</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will not assume that because they see "A/C" on a thermostat, that means they have A/C.</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will not allow the home to become infested with roaches, fleas or bed bugs</p>

<ul data-rte-list="default">
 	<li>
<p class="">Or if they do, they will report it</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will not store personal items on the bare floor of an unfinished damp basement</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will take steps to protect pipes from freezing during very cold weather</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will not steal or destroy window screens, garage door opener remotes, and fan remotes</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They know how to set up mail forwarding</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will check their mail</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They won't allow cable or satellite TV installers to damage the property</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They won't allow friends or family members to bring pets over for short or long term visits</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They won't miss appointments that they themselves scheduled for work they requested</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They change their mind about a lease renewal or moveout date</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will transfer the utilities into their name when they move in</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They understand what "as-is" means</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will not lose their keys</p>

<ul data-rte-list="default">
 	<li>
<p class="">Or if they do, they will solve this problem without your help, especially on a night or weekend</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will not set your property on fire deliberately</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will report chipping or peeling paint and will not allow their children to eat paint chips</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will not put their rent into escrow without notifying you or giving you a chance to remedy</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will not call the health department</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will not call HUD with a false fair housing complaint about you</p>
</li>
 	<li>
<p class="">They will not call the local TV news and lie about you on camera</p>
</li>
</ul>
