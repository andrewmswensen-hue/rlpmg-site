<ul>

</li>
 	<li aria-level="1">Utility minimums and insurance premiums still apply.
</li>

</li>
</ul>
In Columbus, the average rental sits empty for 30–45 days if not aggressively marketed and maintained. That could easily cost you <b>$1,500–$2,250 in lost rent</b>, not including turnover costs.
