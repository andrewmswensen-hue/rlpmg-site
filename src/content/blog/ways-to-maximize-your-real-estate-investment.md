That means even if you do nothing (other than maintain the property), its value will increase and you will generate monthly rental income so long as you keep it consistently occupied. To truly maximize your investment, however, there are several steps you can take.</p>

<h2><strong>Skill to Succeed in Real Estate</strong></h2>
<p>Growing your real estate portfolio can be a lifelong endeavor. As you build your expertise in investing, here are some skills to focus on. Mastering each of these areas will help you make more strategic investment decisions and grow your existing portfolio.</p>

<ul>
 	<li><strong>Financial Skills </strong>- To go far in real estate, you have to become a master of finance and investing. Understanding market conditions, a property's growth potential, interest rates, and how your goals align with your budget will all influence your investment approach. It's also important to become very comfortable with numbers. It's more than just evaluating your financial commitment to invest, you also need to determine how you're going to finance other ongoing parts of your investment property, including taxes, maintenance costs and other operational expenses.</li>
</ul>
<p>When you're not actively calculating costs for a specific property, shore up your skills by learning everything you can about finance and investment strategies.</p>

<ul>
 	<li><strong>Interpersonal Skills </strong>- Relational development may not be the first thing you think about when it comes to real estate growth, but filling your rental units and expanding your portfolio will require you to become a master of relationships and sales. Take steps to build up a strong network with fellow investors and like-minded developers. These relationships will pay dividends when it comes to your long-term real estate investment goals and strategy.</li>
 	<li><strong>Property Management Competence </strong>- Whether you self-manage your rental properties or <u><a href="/blog/should-you-sell-self-manage-or-hire-a-property-management-company/">hire a property management company to do it for you,</a></u> the more you know about management, the more successful you will be with your investment.</li>
 	<li><strong>Property Development Expertise</strong> - Successfully engaging in property development is more than just seeing through a real estate investment strategy. Property developers must have the relational skills to buy and sell properties, the financial backing to fund it, and the property management know-how to revive existing properties or develop them from the ground up so they are rent-ready.</li>
</ul>
<p>As you identify investment goals and look for your next opportunities, keep these skills in mind and take time to learn what you can to improve them.</p>
