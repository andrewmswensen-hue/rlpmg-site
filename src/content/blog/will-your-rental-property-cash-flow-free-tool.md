This post will review estimating the income and expenses of your rental property to determine your anticipated cash flow.  You can download a helpful template to summarize your numbers at the following link: <a href="/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Property-Proforma-Template.pdf">Property Proforma Template</a>

Below is an outline of the figures you will be pulling together on the template.  We've provided guidance on each line item.
<ol>
 	<li>Income
<ul>
 	<li>Income could include Rent, Utilities, Repairs, Parking, Laundry, Water/Sewer, Utility, etc.</li>
 	<li>Deduct anticipated vacancy loss, typically 4-8%.</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>Expenses Incurred <strong>Before</strong> Monthly Property Manager Disbursement
<ul>
 	<li>Capital Spending - Appliances, Furnaces, Air Conditioners when the need replaced</li>
 	<li>Repairs Ongoing - Maintenance requests from tenants</li>
 	<li>Turns - This includes all the expenses incurred while "turning over" the unit between tenants and will include costs due to needed repairs, tenant damages and tenant wear and tear</li>
 	<li>Maintenance Recurring - This is the cost of gutter cleaning, mowing</li>
 	<li>Utilities - If you incur regular utilities costs</li>
 	<li>Property Management Fees - The total cost of all fees charged by your property manager</li>
 	<li>Legal - An estimate of anticipated legal fees, evictions, tenant disputes.</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>Expenses Incurred <strong>After</strong> Monthly Disbursement (To arrive at your Net Operating Income, NOI)
<ul>
 	<li>Association dues</li>
 	<li>Travel costs related to the property</li>
 	<li>Property Taxes (Be sure to estimate the property taxes based on the current market value of the property)</li>
 	<li>Capital Reserves (Money set aside for future large capital needs such as roofs, windows, siding, driveways, etc.)</li>
 	<li>Accounting costs related to the property (This could include tax prep, but also could include any other professional services obtained to help operate the property investment.)</li>
 	<li>Insurance (Property insurance, commonly known as a rental dwelling policy, RDP)</li>
</ul>
</li>
 	<li>Calculating Cash Flow
<ul>
 	<li>After calculating your net operating income (NOI) above, you will now deduct any Principal and Interest payments you have on the property.</li>
 	<li>This will determine your anticipated cash flow.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
