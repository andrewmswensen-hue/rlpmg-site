<h2>Why Leasing Fees Don't Make Sense</h2>
If these<b> leasing fees seem counterintuitive</b>, you're right. A property manager should be motivated to find and place quality residents quickly <i>without</i> additional fees. Why? Without great renters in place, you (the property owner) will miss out on rental income, which means property managers risk losing you as a client. However, traditional property management pricing models that include leasing fees create an <b>incentive for frequent resident turnover</b> rather than <b>long-term retention</b>. 

If a management company profits <b>every time they place a new renter</b>, there's <b>less motivation to focus on resident satisfaction, lease renewals, and long-term stability</b>. Instead, landlords get stuck in a costly cycle of resident churn, leading to <b>higher vacancy rates, more wear and tear on the property, and unnecessary leasing costs.</b>

At RL Property Management, we take a <b>different approach</b>, one that aligns our interests with yours. By eliminating leasing fees, we focus on placing <b>high-quality, long-term residents</b> and keeping <b>your cash flow steady</b> instead of benefiting from frequent turnover.
<h2>Leasing Fees vs. Lease Renewal Fees</h2>
It's important not to confuse leasing fees with <i>lease renewal fees</i>–they sound similar but serve completely different purposes and are <i>much</i> different in cost. 

Most property management companies charge a <b>lease renewal fee</b> when a resident stays for another term. These fees typically range from <b>$200-$400</b> and cover the administrative work required to:
<ul>
 	<li aria-level="1">Prepare updated lease documents</li>
 	<li aria-level="1">Adjust lease terms and rental rates</li>
 	<li aria-level="1">Ensure compliance with local regulations</li>
</ul>
In short, a lease renewal fee is a one-time administrative cost that keeps a good resident in place, whereas a leasing fee is an upfront charge just to fill a vacancy. And unlike leasing fees, <b>lease renewal fees are standard across the industry</b> and cover necessary paperwork and legal compliance.

At RL Property Management, we charge a lease renewal fee, but because our flat-fee model eliminates leasing fees, property owners <b>still come out ahead financially</b> compared to traditional models.
