<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-15522 alignright" src="https://rlpmg.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/pexels-quenani-leal-721480-300x199.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="199" />Just because you're small doesn't mean you can't be mighty. There are many apartment amenities that even small apartment buildings can add or enhance to attract new residents. Here's a run-down of a few popular features to include if you're looking to draw in more tenant interest.</p>

<ul>
 	<li><strong>Enhance technology features</strong> - Ensuring you have robust WiFi connections is important, as is boosting other tech features where feasible. And of course, accepting online rent payments is a must.</li>
 	<li><strong>Add package lockers</strong> - Streamline package delivery to your tenants with a smart locker system for your apartment building.</li>
 	<li><strong>Make it pet-friendly</strong> - If there's one easy thing you can do to attract a broader range of tenants, it's accept pets. Just make sure you have your <a href="/blog/yay-or-nay-to-pets-what-to-include-in-your-lease-agreement/"><u>pet policy clearly spelled out in your lease agreement</u>.</a></li>
 	<li><strong>Upgrade security features</strong> - Offer tenants peace of mind with enhanced security measures in and around your building, including your parking area.</li>
 	<li><strong>Consider adding a gym</strong> - Even if it's a smaller area with a few basic pieces of exercise equipment, having an onsite place to workout is a bonus feature many residents appreciate.</li>
 	<li><strong>Offer storage units </strong>- Residents often have more "stuff" than they have room for in their unit. By offering additional storage, you can attract more tenants to your property.</li>
</ul>
<p>A good resident experience doesn't mean having the biggest or best. It's doing the little things right. Apartment amenities don't have to cost a lot of money or take up a lot of space, but they can make a big difference to a tenant.
