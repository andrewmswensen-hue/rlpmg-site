<h3>We help real estate investors unlock free time and peace of mind by correctly managing their property, transforming their investment into true passive income.</h3>
<h4>What this means in practice:</h4>
<ul>
 	<li>We manage properties for the long term. Our decisions are made assuming the current owner will be holding onto the property for 10 years or longer.</li>
 	<li>We believe in using high-quality materials at every opportunity.</li>
 	<li>We believe in fixing it correctly the first time, regardless of the cost.</li>
 	<li>We believe in paying contractors and vendors fair market prices and not wasting their time with competitive bids or multiple quotes (unless appropriate).</li>
 	<li>We empower our employees to make decisions, and trust them implicitly.</li>
 	<li>We believe incentives are powerful. We are careful about what we incentivize and have structured our business to align our incentives with the best interest of the property owner.</li>
 	<li>Tenant turnover (turns) are expensive and destructive, and should be avoided as much as possible. The goal is LOW tenant turnover; under 30% annually.</li>
 	<li>The market (and no one else) determines the rent amount that a property will lease for.</li>
 	<li>Setting rents at market or slightly below will attract high-quality tenants who are well qualified.</li>
 	<li>Strict and fair tenant screening is integral to keeping turnover rates low.</li>
 	<li>It is better to let a property sit vacant than to lease it to bad tenant.</li>
 	<li>It is better to lower the asking rent than to let a property sit vacant for an extended period of time.</li>
 	<li>Even the best tenant screening is not perfect; bad tenants are an inherent risk of owning rental property.</li>
 	<li>Capital expenses are a normal and expected part of owning rental property. Property owners should be well-capitalized and mentally and financially ready to spend large amounts of money to keep their property in excellent condition.</li>
 	<li>We do not manage vacant units unless they are actively being turned over and made ready to rent.</li>
 	<li>Tenants should be held financially accountable for issues at the property, which are their responsibility per the lease.</li>
 	<li>Strict enforcement of lease terms and company policy, with no exceptions or negotiation, sets the correct tone for a successful tenant relationship.</li>
 	<li>We believe correcting all functional items at a rental property before advertising it for rent is the best way to control long-term costs.</li>
 	<li>We believe attractive and up-to-date finishes are important to today's renter and necessary to secure high quality tenants.</li>
 	<li>Prevention is cheaper than the cure. Proactive property maintenance is a must.</li>
 	<li>We do not provide play-by-play status updates to property owners; instead we outline our approach and let the system work.</li>
 	<li>Property becomes an "Asset" when leased; emotional attachment and high expectations should be removed from the situation.</li>
</ul>
