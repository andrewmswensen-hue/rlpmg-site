For new investors and existing property owners alike, the rental market presents a steady source of income and even greater opportunities to increase the property's value through strategic efforts that boost NOI, or net operating income.</p>

<h2><strong>Ways to Increase a Rental Property's Value</strong></h2>
<p>Unlike some investment opportunities that are left to the whims of the market, investors in real estate have at least <a href="/blog/5-smart-decisions-that-boost-noi/"><u>some direct control</u> </a>over how their property is valued. There are actionable steps they can take to increase income and reduce expenses - and thereby boost the property's NOI.</p>
<p>Whether you own single-family or multifamily properties, the <a href="/blog/how-to-improve-your-propertys-noi/"><u>steps you take to improve NOI</u></a> are generally the same. Here are some key areas to focus on:</p>

<ul>
 	<li>Reducing operating expenses</li>
 	<li>Controlling maintenance costs</li>
 	<li>Securing and retaining good tenants</li>
 	<li>Ensuring rent rates are in line with the market</li>
 	<li>Auditing your income and expenses (and standardizing and streamlining fees wherever possible)</li>
 	<li>Continually monitoring your data and adjusting as necessary</li>
</ul>
