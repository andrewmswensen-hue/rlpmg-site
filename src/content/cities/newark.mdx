## What is it like to own a rental in Newark?

Newark is a city in Licking County, roughly 41 miles east-northeast of downtown Columbus, about a 70-minute drive. Population is approximately 49,934. Local anchors include Newark Earthworks (the largest earthwork complex in the Ohio River Valley, UNESCO World Heritage listed), Ohio State University at Newark and Central Ohio Technical College, The Works: Ohio Center for History, Art and Technology, and Licking County Courthouse.

## What should an owner know about the Newark rental market?

Licking County seat and Ohio's 18th-largest city, roughly 40 miles east of downtown Columbus. The headline for owners is rent growth: about 8.7% year over year, the fastest on this list, driven by Intel and the New Albany-area industrial buildout spilling east into Licking County. Entry prices remain low relative to Franklin County, so this is where the yield is, but median household income is only about $52,570, so tenant affordability is a real constraint on how far rents can run. Ohio State Newark and Central Ohio Technical College share a campus with roughly 5,800 combined students, supporting a student and staff rental segment. The Newark Earthworks earned UNESCO World Heritage listing. No municipal source-of-income ordinance. Licking County is under 200,000 residents, so the ORC 5323 county auditor rental registration requirement does not apply here the way it does in Franklin County.

## Who rents in Newark?

Renter demand in Newark tracks the local drivers: families choosing Newark City School District, Licking Valley Local School District (portions of the area), and Granville Exempted Village Schools (adjacent), students and university staff, employees of nearby major employers, including corporate relocations, and professionals who want to be close to downtown. Screening applies the same published standard everywhere RL Property Management operates: household income of at least 3 times the monthly rent, no evictions in the past 5 years, no violent criminal convictions, and verified rental history, applied consistently under federal, Ohio, and applicable local fair housing law.
