## What is it like to own a rental in Marysville?

Marysville is a city in Union County, roughly 35 miles northwest of downtown Columbus, about a 60-minute drive. Population is approximately 25,571. Local landmarks and anchors include Scotts Miracle-Gro world headquarters, Honda Marysville Auto Plant (about six miles northwest), Ohio Reformatory for Women, and Union County Airport. Union County seat and a true company town in the best sense: Honda's Marysville Auto Plant (the lead Accord plant since 1982) sits about six miles northwest, and Scotts Miracle-Gro is headquartered in the city, with a Nestle technology center as well. That means a large, stable, shift-working tenant base with reliable W-2 income, and rental demand that tracks Honda's production schedule and the Honda/LG battery investment in the corridor. Population grew 15.7% between 2010 and 2020. Marysville Exempted Village Schools serves most of the city. No municipal source-of-income ordinance. Note the distance: roughly a 35-40 minute drive to downtown Columbus, so this is a local-employment rental market, not a Columbus commuter market.
