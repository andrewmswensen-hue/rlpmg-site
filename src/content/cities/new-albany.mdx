## What is it like to own a rental in New Albany?

New Albany is a city in Franklin County and Licking County, roughly 16 miles northeast of downtown Columbus, about a 27-minute drive. Population is approximately 10,825. Local landmarks and anchors include New Albany International Business Park, Intel's Ohio semiconductor fabrication campus (Licking County side), Jeanne B. McCoy Community Center for the Arts, and Rose Run Park and the New Albany Leisure Trail. The most important rental story in Central Ohio right now. Intel began construction in September 2022 on what was announced as the world's largest semiconductor fabrication site, on roughly 926 acres annexed into New Albany on the Licking County side, alongside a wave of data-center and advanced-manufacturing investment in the New Albany International Business Park (Abercrombie & Fitch, Bob Evans, Discover Financial, Amazon Web Services, Google). Expect sustained demand for executive and corporate-relocation rentals plus heavy pressure on nearby Licking County and Blacklick housing. Rents are the highest of any place on this list at about $1,739 average, and median household income is roughly $203,000. Practical cautions: the city spans two counties (different tax rates and filing venues), most neighborhoods are governed by strict HOAs and architectural covenants that restrict leasing, and district assignment splits between New Albany-Plain and Licking Heights. No municipal source-of-income ordinance.
