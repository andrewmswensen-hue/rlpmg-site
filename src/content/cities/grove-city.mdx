## What is it like to own a rental in Grove City?

Grove City is a city in Franklin County, roughly 9 miles southwest of downtown Columbus, about a 15-minute drive. Population is approximately 41,252. Local anchors include Grove City Town Center and Broadway, Beulah Park (former racetrack, now a mixed-use neighborhood), Scioto Grove Metro Park, and Gantz Homestead and Herb Gardens (1832, National Register).

## What should an owner know about the Grove City rental market?

Largest southwest-side suburb and one of the strongest workforce-rental markets in Franklin County: solid rents against comparatively low acquisition prices. The whole city is in South-Western City Schools, so unlike most Central Ohio suburbs there is no district-boundary premium to manage within the city. Employment demand comes from the Rickenbacker inland port and the I-71/I-270 southwest logistics corridor (distribution and manufacturing), plus Grove City Methodist Hospital. Beulah Park redevelopment and continued single-family construction keep new supply coming, which caps rent growth relative to the north side. No municipal source-of-income ordinance.
