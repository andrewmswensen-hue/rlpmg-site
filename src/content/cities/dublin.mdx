## What is it like to own a rental in Dublin?

Dublin is a city in Franklin County, with portions in Delaware, Union, and Madison counties, roughly 16 miles northwest of downtown Columbus, about a 27-minute drive. Population is approximately 49,328. Local landmarks and anchors include Muirfield Village Golf Club (host of the Memorial Tournament), Field of Corn public art installation, Historic Dublin and the Bridge Park development, and Scioto Park and the Leatherlips sculpture. High-end northwest suburb spanning four counties, which means multiple tax districts and county filing venues for the same city. Dublin City Schools is the single biggest rent driver in the market. Top employers are Cardinal Health (about 4,800), OhioHealth (about 2,000), Dublin City Schools (about 1,951), OCLC and Wendy's corporate, and there is a large Japanese expatriate community tied to Honda's regional operations, which produces reliable corporate-relocation tenants on 12-to-24-month terms. Bridge Park has added significant Class A apartment supply that competes with single-family rentals at the top of the market. Many neighborhoods sit under active HOAs with rental caps and leasing restrictions, verify HOA rules before taking on a Dublin listing.
