## What is it like to own a rental in Ashville?

Ashville is a village in Pickaway County, roughly 21 miles south of downtown Columbus, about a 36-minute drive. Population is approximately 4,529. Local anchors include Ohio's Small Town Museum (home of the world's oldest working traffic light), Ashville Depot (1876, National Register of Historic Places), Old Town Jail Museum, and Ashville Viking Festival grounds.

## What should an owner know about the Ashville rental market?

Small Pickaway County village on the far south edge of the Columbus commuter shed, growing fast off new subdivision construction along US-23. Teays Valley schools are the main draw for renting families. Rental stock is mostly single-family and duplex; no apartment-market rent index is published for the village, so pricing has to be pulled from comparable Pickaway County listings. Rickenbacker Inland Port and the Scioto/US-23 logistics corridor to the north are the dominant employment pull for tenants here.
