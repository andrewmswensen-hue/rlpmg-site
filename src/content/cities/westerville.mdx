## What is it like to own a rental in Westerville?

Westerville is a city in Franklin County and Delaware County, roughly 15 miles north-northeast of downtown Columbus, about a 26-minute drive. Population is approximately 39,190. Local anchors include Otterbein University, Uptown Westerville State Street district, Hanby House (Underground Railroad station, home of composer Benjamin Hanby), and Anti-Saloon League Museum, Westerville was the Dry Capital of the World.

## What should an owner know about the Westerville rental market?

Large northeast suburb spanning the Franklin/Delaware county line, with a genuine employment base of its own: JPMorgan Chase (about 3,471 employees in 2023), Mount Carmel Health System (about 3,293) and Westerville City Schools (about 1,836). Otterbein University, with over 3,000 students, supports a steady student and faculty rental segment around Uptown. Westerville City Schools runs three high schools (North, South, Central) and district assignment matters to family tenants. Westerville was one of the earliest Central Ohio suburbs to adopt a source-of-income ordinance (2020), so voucher screening rules have applied here longer than in most of the region. Proximity to Polaris and the I-270/State Route 3 corridor keeps demand steady.
