## What is it like to own a rental in Pickerington?

Pickerington is a city in Fairfield County and Franklin County, roughly 17 miles east-southeast of downtown Columbus, about a 29-minute drive. Population is approximately 23,094. Local landmarks and anchors include AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Museum, Pickerington Ponds Metro Park, OhioHealth Pickerington Methodist Hospital (opened December 2023), and Olde Pickerington Village. Fast-growing southeast suburb, the second-largest city in Fairfield County, and one of the two Central Ohio suburbs that most owners are surprised to learn has a source-of-income ordinance (passed 2024), update your screening criteria before advertising here. Pickerington Local Schools runs 15 buildings including two high schools (North and Central) and is the dominant rent driver; the district also extends into surrounding townships beyond city limits. The city straddles the Fairfield/Franklin county line, so confirm the county before quoting taxes or filing an eviction. OhioHealth's Pickerington Methodist Hospital opened in December 2023, adding a local healthcare employment base to what was previously a pure commuter market on US-33 and I-70.
