## What is it like to own a rental in Clintonville?

Clintonville is a Columbus neighborhood in Franklin County, roughly 7 miles north of downtown Columbus. Population is approximately 30,000. Local anchors include Whetstone Park and the Columbus Park of Roses, Whetstone High School, Graceland Shopping Center, and Olentangy Trail along the Olentangy River.

## What should an owner know about the Clintonville rental market?

North-central Columbus neighborhood bounded by the Olentangy River, Glen Echo Creek, the rail line, and Henderson Road. Sits directly north of Ohio State, so graduate students, hospital staff, and young professionals are the core tenant pool, but unlike the campus fringe, Clintonville leans toward long-tenure renters in single-family homes and doubles. Housing stock is largely 1910s-1950s, so knob-and-tube wiring, original plumbing, and slate/asbestos siding show up in inspections. The Clintonville Area Commission reviews zoning and demolition requests. As part of Columbus, the source-of-income ordinance and the city's rental rules apply.
