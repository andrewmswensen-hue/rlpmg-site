## What is it like to own a rental in Upper Arlington?

Upper Arlington is a city in Franklin County, roughly 7 miles northwest of downtown Columbus, about a 12-minute drive. Population is approximately 36,800. Local landmarks and anchors include Upper Arlington Historic District (National Register, 1985, covering the southern half of the city), Scioto Country Club, Jack Nicklaus Park, and Jones Middle School (designed by Ohio Stadium architect Howard Dwight Smith). Affluent inner-ring city on the northwest side, bordering Ohio State and the Scioto River. Upper Arlington City Schools, with International Baccalaureate programming, is the primary rent driver and the school board has historically been the city's largest employer. Rental inventory is scarce and heavily single-family: this is an owner-occupant city, so the rental niche is OSU faculty, medical professionals at nearby OSU Wexner and Riverside, and relocating executives on one-to-two-year terms. Housing stock is largely 1920s-1960s with the southern half inside a National Register historic district. Upper Arlington passed a source-of-income ordinance in 2024, which many owners and agents have not caught up to.
