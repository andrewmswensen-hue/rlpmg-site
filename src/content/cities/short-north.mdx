## What is it like to own a rental in Short North?

Short North is a Columbus neighborhood in Franklin County, roughly 2 miles north-northwest of downtown Columbus. Local landmarks and anchors include The 17 lighted metal arches over High Street, Greater Columbus Convention Center, Goodale Park, and North Market. Columbus's densest walkable rental submarket, running along High Street from Goodale Street north to King Avenue, between Victorian Village and Italian Village and connecting downtown to Ohio State. Tenant profile is young professionals, grad students and medical residents rather than families, so expect shorter average tenancies, high turnover, and rents that are premium per square foot but on small units. Parking is the number one leasing objection and a dedicated space materially raises achievable rent. Housing stock is early-1900s brick, much of it in Columbus historic districts (Victorian Village and Italian Village commissions review exterior work on adjacent blocks), and a large volume of newer Class A apartments now competes directly. Nightlife noise and short-term-rental activity are real management issues here, Columbus Chapter 598 permitting applies to any rental under 30 days.

## Who rents in Short North?

Renter demand in Short North tracks the local drivers: families choosing Columbus City Schools and students and university staff. Screening applies the same published standard everywhere RL Property Management operates: household income of at least 3 times the monthly rent, no evictions in the past 5 years, no violent criminal convictions, and verified rental history.
