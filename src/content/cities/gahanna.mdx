## What is it like to own a rental in Gahanna?

Gahanna is a city in Franklin County, roughly 10 miles east-northeast of downtown Columbus, about a 17-minute drive. Population is approximately 35,726. Local anchors include Creekside Gahanna (shops, park and amphitheater on Big Walnut Creek), Big Walnut Creek and the Gahanna Woods State Nature Preserve, Central Park of Gahanna, and The Log House (Gahanna Historical Society).

## What should an owner know about the Gahanna rental market?

Northeast Franklin County suburb built along Big Walnut Creek, directly adjacent to John Glenn International Airport, airport, air-cargo and logistics employment is a major tenant source, along with the Easton commercial district just to the west. Gahanna-Jefferson schools support solid family rental demand. Gahanna has its own source-of-income ordinance (2022), so voucher screening rules are stricter than surrounding unincorporated township land. Housing stock is largely 1960s-1990s single-family with a growing townhome segment around Creekside.
