## What is it like to own a rental in Hilliard?

Hilliard is a city in Franklin County, roughly 12 miles northwest of downtown Columbus, about a 20-minute drive. Population is approximately 37,114. Local landmarks and anchors include Old Hilliard historic business district, Early Television Museum, First Responders Park (with a World Trade Center steel memorial), and Franklin County Fairgrounds and Historical Village. Fast-growing northwest suburb whose main rental draw is Hilliard City Schools, and the district extends well beyond the city limits into parts of Columbus, Dublin and Galloway, so plenty of Columbus-address homes sell and rent at a Hilliard-schools premium. That mismatch cuts both ways: a Hilliard mailing address does not guarantee Hilliard schools. Three high schools (Bradley, Darby, Davidson) split the district and families do care which one. Employment demand comes from the I-270 west corridor, Mount Carmel and the Dublin/Tuttle office parks. Newer stock (1990s-2020s) means lower maintenance drag than the inner ring. No municipal source-of-income ordinance as of August 2026.
