## What is it like to own a rental in Powell?

Powell is a city in Delaware County, roughly 19 miles north-northwest of downtown Columbus, about a 32-minute drive. Population is approximately 14,163. Local anchors include Columbus Zoo and Aquarium and Zoombezi Bay (just west, in Liberty Township), Village Green Park and the Powell Festival, Adventure Park, and Seldom Seen Park.

## What should an owner know about the Powell rental market?

Affluent Delaware County city north of Columbus with the highest average apartment rent of any Columbus suburb RentCafe tracks (about $1,943). The entire city is in Olentangy Local Schools, which is the single strongest school brand in Central Ohio real estate and the main reason tenants pay a premium here. Population grew 23.2% from 2010 to 2020. Inventory for rent is thin: this is an owner-occupant market, and most rental product is single-family homes in HOA-governed subdivisions, so verify leasing restrictions and rental caps before signing an owner. The Columbus Zoo and Aquarium sits just west in Liberty Township. No municipal source-of-income ordinance. Delaware County property taxes and the Delaware County auditor apply, not Franklin County.
