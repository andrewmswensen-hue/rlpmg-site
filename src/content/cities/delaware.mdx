## What is it like to own a rental in Delaware?

Delaware is a city in Delaware County, roughly 28 miles north of downtown Columbus, about a 48-minute drive. Population is approximately 41,302. Local landmarks and anchors include Ohio Wesleyan University, Methodist Theological School in Ohio, Historic Northwest District (500+ homes on the National Register), and Delaware County Fairgrounds and the Little Brown Jug harness race. County seat of Delaware County, one of the fastest-growing counties in Ohio, and far enough north that it functions as its own small city rather than a bedroom suburb. Ohio Wesleyan University supplies a steady student and faculty rental pool near downtown, while the Historic Northwest District's 500-plus National Register homes carry preservation expectations on exterior work. The US-23 corridor and Polaris-area employment to the south pull commuters. Note the district split: a Delaware mailing address may sit in Delaware City, Buckeye Valley, or Olentangy schools, and Olentangy commands a rent premium.
