## What is it like to own a rental in Columbus?

Columbus is a city in Franklin County (county seat), with portions in Delaware County and Fairfield County, columbus is the city itself; distances on this site are measured from downtown Columbus at Broad and High streets. Population is approximately 905,748. Local anchors include Ohio Statehouse, Ohio State University and Ohio Stadium, COSI, and Franklin Park Conservatory.

## What should an owner know about the Columbus rental market?

Ohio's largest city and the 14th largest in the US, and the core of each Central Ohio rental strategy. Franklin County is the primary jurisdiction, but Columbus city limits also spill into Delaware and Fairfield counties, and city limits interlock with more than a dozen suburbs, a Columbus mailing address does not mean a parcel is in the city, and vice versa. School district matters enormously: seven districts serve parts of the city, and a Columbus City Schools address rents very differently from a Hilliard or Dublin district address at the same price point. Ohio State University (roughly 60,000 students), OhioHealth, Nationwide, Cardinal Health, JPMorgan Chase, AEP and Huntington anchor tenant demand. Columbus has both a source-of-income ordinance and a short-term rental permit regime, the two most common compliance traps for out-of-area owners.
