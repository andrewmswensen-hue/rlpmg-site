## What is it like to own a rental in London?

London is a city in Madison County, roughly 30 miles west-southwest of downtown Columbus, about a 51-minute drive. Population is approximately 10,279. Local anchors include Madison County Courthouse, Madison County Airport, First United Methodist Church (London's first church building, 1820), and London City Park.

## What should an owner know about the London rental market?

Madison County seat southwest of Columbus on I-70 and US-42. Small, affordable, and increasingly pulled into the Columbus commuter and industrial orbit: the I-70 west corridor, Honda's Union County operations to the north, and the Madison County logistics/data-center development wave are the employment story to watch. RentCafe publishes no apartment-market average for London, so comps must be built from local single-family and small-multifamily listings. Rental stock is mostly older single-family and converted doubles near downtown. Owners should expect a thinner contractor bench and longer vendor response times than inside I-270.
