## What is it like to own a rental in Grandview Heights?

Grandview Heights is a city in Franklin County, roughly 3 miles west-northwest of downtown Columbus, about a 10-minute drive. Population is approximately 8,085. Local anchors include Grandview Yard mixed-use development, The Bank Block (1927 historic strip retail on Grandview Avenue), Grandview Heights High School, and Pierce Field.

## What should an owner know about the Grandview Heights rental market?

Tiny (1.32 sq mi) inner-ring city completely surrounded by Columbus and Marble Cliff, immediately west of the Scioto. It is one of the highest-demand rental submarkets in Central Ohio on a per-square-foot basis: walkable to the Grandview Yard employment district, minutes from OSU and downtown, and served by its own small, well-regarded Grandview Heights City Schools. Housing stock is overwhelmingly 1910s-1930s, and lots are small with limited off-street parking. Grandview Heights passed a source-of-income ordinance in 2024, which is newer than most owners realize and often missed in screening policies. Inventory is very tight, almost nothing new is built, so turnover, not construction, sets the market.
