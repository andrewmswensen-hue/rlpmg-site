## What is it like to own a rental in Bexley?

Bexley is a city in Franklin County, roughly 4 miles east of downtown Columbus, about a 10-minute drive. Population is approximately 13,928. Local anchors include Ohio Governor's Residence, Capital University, Jeffrey Mansion and Jeffrey Park, and Drexel Theatre.

## What should an owner know about the Bexley rental market?

Affluent, fully built-out inner-ring city surrounded by Columbus on all sides, and the first US city to earn arboretum accreditation. Bexley City Schools and Capital University drive rental demand: large older homes near campus, plus a steady faculty/professional tenant pool. Housing stock skews pre-1940, so maintenance reserves matter. Bexley has its own source-of-income ordinance, so voucher screening rules differ from unincorporated Franklin County. RentCafe publishes no apartment-market average for Bexley, so comps must come from single-family listings.
