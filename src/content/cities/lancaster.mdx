## What is it like to own a rental in Lancaster?

Lancaster is a city in Fairfield County, roughly 32 miles southeast of downtown Columbus, about a 54-minute drive. Population is approximately 40,552. Local landmarks and anchors include The Sherman House (birthplace of Gen. William Tecumseh Sherman), Ohio Glass Museum, The Georgian Museum (1832 Federal-style home), and Rising Park and Mount Pleasant. Fairfield County seat and a genuine small city rather than a bedroom suburb, it has its own hospital, manufacturing base and downtown. Top employers are Fairfield Medical Center (about 2,314), Anchor Hocking (about 1,782), Fairfield County government, Lancaster City Schools and Kroger. Entry prices are the lowest of any place on this list, and rents ($1,268 average, up nearly 4% year over year) support strong cash-on-cash returns, but tenant screening and turnover management matter more here than in the affluent north-side suburbs. Three National Register historic districts downtown carry exterior-work expectations on older properties. About a 35-minute drive to downtown Columbus on US-33.
