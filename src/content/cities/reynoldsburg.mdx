## What is it like to own a rental in Reynoldsburg?

Reynoldsburg is a city in Franklin County, with portions in Licking County and Fairfield County, roughly 14 miles east of downtown Columbus, about a 24-minute drive. Population is approximately 41,076. Local landmarks and anchors include Blacklick Woods Metro Park (643 acres, with golf course), Alexander W. Livingston House museum, Annual Tomato Festival, Reynoldsburg calls itself the Birthplace of the Tomato, and Reynoldsburg High School Livingston and Summit campuses. East-side suburb spanning three counties (Franklin, Licking, Fairfield), so tax rates, auditor filings and eviction venue vary by parcel, confirm the parcel. One of the best value-oriented rental markets close to I-270: average rent about $1,381 against relatively low acquisition prices, with strong workforce tenant demand. Top employers are Victoria's Secret Service Company (about 3,024), Reynoldsburg City Schools and Kroger. Reynoldsburg has had a source-of-income ordinance since 2021, so voucher applicants cannot be screened out on that basis. The Licking Heights portion of the city is being reshaped by Intel-driven growth to the northeast. Housing stock is mostly 1950s-1990s single-family plus a large apartment inventory along Brice Road.
